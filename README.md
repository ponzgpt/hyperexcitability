# Muscle hyperexcitability atlas

A single-page, interactive study atlas of the EURO-NMD Basics webinar [*Muscle hyperexcitability and cramps*](https://youtu.be/Do0uAotLGow) by Dr Federica Montagnese.

Live: https://hyperexcitability.technoir.cloud

It organises the talk on three axes (where the hyperexcitability starts, what the EMG hears, why it happens) and pairs each entity with a small interactive model: an origin map, a sign → EMG → cause graph, an excitation–contraction stepper, a firing-rate ruler, synthesised EMG sounds, and a quiz.

Study notes, not medical advice. Items marked `bg` are standard background rather than claims from the talk.
